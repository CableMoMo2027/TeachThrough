# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Parents, students, and tutors looking for educational matching. Users value reliability, ease of use, and a professional yet welcoming atmosphere.

## Product Purpose
A platform to find and connect with the right tutors based on subjects, levels, price, and teaching style. It aims to bridge the gap between quality educators and learners with a seamless, high-end experience.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, Modern, Friendly, Luxurious, Sharp, Simple (น่าเชื่อถือ, ทันสมัย, เป็นกันเอง, หรูหรา, เฉียบคม, เรียบง่าย).

## Anti-references
Avoid cluttered interfaces, generic SaaS templates with garish gradients, and poorly spaced layouts that feel "cheap" or overwhelming.

## Design Principles
- **Crafted Precision**: Every detail (shadows, borders, icons) should feel intentional and refined.
- **Fluid Simplicity**: Complex flows (like tutor registration) should feel effortless through smooth transitions and clear hierarchy.
- **Expert Warmth**: Use professional typography and "sharp" layouts balanced with friendly, approachable colors and micro-interactions.
- **Elevated Clarity**: Prioritize readability and "breathable" spacing to convey a sense of luxury and calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.
- High contrast for readability.
- Support for reduced motion for users who prefer it.

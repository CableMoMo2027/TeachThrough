# Tutor Finding

This context defines the product language for matching learners with tutors and onboarding tutors onto the platform.

## Language

**Tutor Recruitment Page**:
A page that explains the value, expectations, and process of becoming a tutor, then directs qualified prospects to start tutor registration.
_Avoid_: Become-a-tutor form, tutor sign-up page

**Tutor Registration**:
The process where a prospective tutor provides the required profile and teaching information to join the platform as a tutor.
_Avoid_: Sign up 2, tutor form

**Tutor Registration Form**:
The focused form experience where a prospective tutor submits the information required for tutor registration.
_Avoid_: Marketing page, become-a-tutor page

**Tutor Application**:
The submitted record that the team reviews after a prospective tutor completes tutor registration.
_Avoid_: Registration, tutor profile

**Tutor Profile**:
The public-facing tutor information that learners use to evaluate and contact a tutor after the tutor has been approved.
_Avoid_: Tutor application, verification record

**Pending Review**:
The initial status of a tutor application before the team has reviewed the submitted information and documents.
_Avoid_: Waiting, submitted, pending

**Teaching Experience**:
Structured evidence of a tutor's prior teaching work, including duration, learner levels, teaching formats, taught subjects, and short outcome examples.
_Avoid_: Bio, description, about me

**Learner Level**:
The education stage or learner group that a tutor can teach, such as primary school, secondary school, university, or adult learners.
_Avoid_: Subject, grade only
